# industry_academy_bridge
This is a bridging program that allows for easier, quicker and better transfer of technology and engineering processes for emerging technologies and topics like AI, QAI, AGI, ASI etc

This program considers key issues and delays that are impeding the transfer of University and R&D labs based technologies to the right company and a t the right time. The program addresses various concerns and delays and offer quick solutions that can help boost the process and economy. 

Various related programs like Applied Research (AR) can help reduce the amount of skilled researchers in the corporate world. AR can help reduce the doctoral level trainings; instead of 5-7 years, AR can be compeleted in just 2-3 years term. This can be a on the job training that can help corproates to adopt emerging technologies leveraging the research work of the AR students. In house programs, on campus visits, and various focus topics, blended to meed the corproate needs are possible. 

Personalised AR programs are a great boon to the industries keen on working towards specific projects. We at Bhadale IT, offer unique blended course advice that can be fulfilled by collaborating with various Universities.
